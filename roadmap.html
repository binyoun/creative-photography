Before you start

Bring a camera that shoots RAWYou need manual control of aperture, shutter speed and ISO. RMIT doesn't lend cameras for this course.
Know the basics alreadyThis is an advanced course. You should be comfortable with the exposure triangle and Lightroom, for example from Photography 101.
Write each journal entry that weekA1 covers the Week 2 to 5 experiments. Write each entry while the work is fresh, not in Week 7.
No AI on your imagesYou may use AI to plan or fix grammar, never to generate, edit or alter images. Reference any AI you use.
Get permission on campusCheck the Taking Photos on Campus page before you shoot around RMIT.
Start your project idea earlyYou choose your own final project with no brief. The topic needs approval by the end of Week 8, so start thinking about it now.

All due times are Vietnam time (ICT). Canvas is the official record: if anything here differs from Canvas, Canvas wins. Feedback on each assignment comes back within two weeks of its due date, in Marks on Canvas.
