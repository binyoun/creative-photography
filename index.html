This semester

Six weeks of experiments that push photography past the single frame, then six weeks developing your own photographic project from idea to final presentation. Everything for the course lives on this page; the official record is always Canvas.

  • SemesterSemester 3, 2026 · Oct 19 to Jan 15
  • CourseVART3670 Creative Photography
  • ClassA 3-hour studio class every week. Weeks 4 and 6 are in Media Studio 1.1.34.
  • WorkloadAbout 6 hours of work a week outside class
  • You needYour own camera that shoots RAW, with manual aperture, shutter speed and ISO
  • BreakChristmas break the week of Dec 21, no class

Weeks 2 to 6

Five experiments

Each week pushes photography past one of its limits. Every experiment becomes one entry in your Visual Journal (A1), so write it up the same week.

Experiment 1 · Week 2 · Oct 26

Beyond the Absence of Colours

Black and white, and white balance in colour photography.

Look at: Robert Frank, the Zone System

Experiment 2 · Week 3 · Nov 2

Beyond the Lens

Databending and glitch art: break the file to make the image.

Look at: glitch effects, the databending tutorial on Canvas

Experiment 3 · Week 4 · Nov 9

Beyond the Site

Creative studio lighting and light painting, in Media Studio 1.1.34. Early feedback from your lecturer this week.

Look at: Picasso drawing with light

Experiment 4 · Week 5 · Nov 16

Beyond Single Frame

Multiple exposure and compositing: build one image out of many.

Look at: identity and layering, Merleau-Ponty on perception

Experiment 5 · Week 6 · Nov 23

Beyond the Medium

Remix between digital and analog: print a photo, alter it by hand, digitise it, edit it, then print and alter it again. In Media Studio 1.1.34; bring your camera.

Try: the Alternative Process Lab and Elemental WebAR below

Weeks 7 to 12

Final project

Your own concept, no brief: 10 to 30 connected images. Start thinking about your idea now, because the topic must be approved by the end of Week 8.

01 Week 7 · Nov 30

Find your project

Study 3 photographers and 3 photo books, choose a topic and a working title, shoot, and bring a first edit of 10 to 20 images.

02 Week 8 · Dec 7

Lock your topic

Present and discuss your topic. Approval from your lecturer by the end of the week. Visit Matca.

03 Weeks 9 to 10

Shoot

1:1 consultations on your project. Bring your camera. Week 10 is Dec 28, after the Christmas break.

04 Week 11 · Jan 4

Final edit

Bring at least 50 printed images of your project, then edit and sequence the series in class.

05 Week 12 · Jan 11

Showcase

Show your final project in class. Attendance is expected.

How you are assessed

Assignments

Submit everything on Canvas. All due times are Vietnam time (ICT).

Visual Journals

One PDF with 4 journal entries, one each for Weeks 2, 3, 4 and 5: 4 edited photos and 250 to 500 words per entry, plus behind-the-scenes material.

Due Fri Dec 4, 22:00
A2 · Weeks 7 to 12 40%

Final Project

Your own concept, no brief: 10 to 30 connected images. Topic approved by the end of Week 8. Shown in class in Week 12.

Due Fri Jan 15, 22:00
A3 · Weeks 7 to 12 20%

Final Project Documentation

750 to 1000 words on your workflow, challenges and how you solved them, with behind-the-scenes images, setups and moodboards.

Due Fri Jan 15, 22:00

Good to know

Rules and tips

  • No AI on your imagesYou may use AI to plan or fix grammar, never to generate, edit or alter images. Reference any AI you use.
  • Know the basics alreadyThis is an advanced course. You should be comfortable with the exposure triangle and Lightroom, for example from Photography 101.
  • Bring your own cameraIt must shoot RAW with manual controls. RMIT doesn't lend cameras for this course.
  • Write each journal entry that weekA1 covers the Week 2 to 5 experiments. Write each entry while the work is fresh, not in Week 7.
  • Get permission on campusCheck the Taking Photos on Campus page on Canvas before you shoot around RMIT.
  • Canvas winsIf anything here differs from Canvas, Canvas is correct. Feedback comes back within two weeks of each due date.
